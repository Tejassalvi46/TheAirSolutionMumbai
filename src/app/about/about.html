ABOUT THE AIR SOLUTIONS

Established in 2017 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, THE AIR SOLUTIONS is a growing name in the air-conditioning industry, providing comprehensive solutions in air-conditioning sales, wholesale distribution, technical execution, servicing, and commercial HVAC projects.

With a strong commitment to quality, integrity, customer satisfaction, and professional service, we have built our reputation through hard work, transparent business practices, and long-term relationships with our customers and business partners.

Our objective is to deliver reliable, energy-efficient, and cost-effective air-conditioning solutions tailored to the unique requirements of residential, commercial, and corporate clients.
